# Product

## Register

product

## Users

**Primary:** Enterprise staff officers and administrators responsible for document quality control workflows in Indonesian institutions. They work at office desks during standard business hours, processing high volumes of documents daily. They need efficient, no-nonsense interfaces to submit documents, review submissions from colleagues, and make approval/rejection decisions quickly with clear audit trails.

## Product Purpose

SISTEM QC BERKAS is an enterprise document quality control management system that streamlines the submission, review, and approval workflow for institutional documents. Success means: documents move through the workflow predictably, staff can see status at a glance, approvers make decisions confidently with all required context, and administrators maintain audit integrity and oversight.

## Brand Personality

**Professional. Efficient. Clear.**

The interface is the tool, not the event. Every pixel serves the workflow. Information is presented without decoration. Actions are direct and consequences are visible. The system earns trust through transparency: staff see exactly what's expected, what's blocked, what's approved. Visual hierarchy is strict; secondary details don't compete with primary decisions.

## Anti-references

- Booring, lifeless grays that drain motivation
- Cluttered interfaces with competing visual hierarchies
- Ambiguous status indicators or unclear next steps
- Decorative flourishes that slow down task completion
- Unclear error messages or cryptic validation states

## Design Principles

1. **Clarity first**: Every state, status, and action is obvious without explanation. Headers, labels, and messages are direct and specific.
2. **Efficiency without sterility**: Professional presentation doesn't mean cold or uninviting. Use breathing room, clean typography, and purposeful color to keep interfaces engaging and human.
3. **Workflow visibility**: Show the user where they are in the process (submission → review → approval/rejection) and what comes next. Make progress obvious.
4. **Respectful of cognitive load**: Minimize options on any screen. Group related actions. Surface only information relevant to the current task.
5. **Confidence in decision-making**: Approvers need full context (attachments, history, pending items) to decide with authority. Reduce friction before the decision point.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- **Level**: WCAG 2.1 AA (standard web accessibility)
- **Motion**: Minimize motion; prioritize clarity and efficiency. No animations on critical paths. No auto-playing content.
- **Language**: UI text is Indonesian. Support for screen readers and keyboard navigation throughout.
- **Color**: Do not rely on color alone to convey status (use icons, text, and badges). Maintain ≥4.5:1 contrast on body text and interactive elements.
- **Responsiveness**: Support desktop/tablet workflows; mobile is secondary but functional (staff may check on phones, but primary work is desktop).
